Privacy

A small static website with minimal data collection.

This site is designed to work without advertising, client-side analytics, tracking pixels, account creation, comments, or a contact form.

Data collected by this website

The public pages do not intentionally collect personal information through forms, accounts, or browser storage. The site does not set first-party marketing or analytics cookies.

Infrastructure logs

Cloudflare may process ordinary request data needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp, and security signals. Retention and processing are governed by the configured Cloudflare account and applicable Cloudflare terms.

External links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, and other external sites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Email

When you email David, the information in that message is processed through the email providers used by the sender and recipient. Do not send secrets, credentials, brokerage information, or sensitive personal data.

Documents

Public résumé and CV files intentionally exclude date of birth, phone number, home address, and application-specific information. Downloadable PDFs are marked not to be indexed so the HTML CV remains the canonical public record.
