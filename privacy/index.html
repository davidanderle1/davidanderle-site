Privacy.

This website does not use visitor analytics, advertising trackers or social media embeds.

There is no visitor account or contact form. Fonts and other website assets are served locally. Following an external link takes you to a separate service with its own privacy practices.

If you contact me by email, the information you send is used to respond to your message. When your browser requests a page, the hosting service receives connection information, including your IP address. This is separate from visitor analytics.
