About

A little about me.

Studying Informatics in Prague. Exploring a future in finance through data, research and code.

I am interested in how financial decisions are made, and how data and computation can make them better.

I am completing a bachelor's in Informatics at Czech University of Life Sciences Prague, expected in 2027, and I am based in Prague.

Finance is the direction I want to take. My technical background gives me a way to explore it: turn a question into a model, examine its assumptions and learn from what holds up.

My current work includes a synthetic model of liquidation feedback and collaborative work on cryptographic commitments. You can explore the projects, my contribution and their limitations in Work.

A wider perspective

I completed a non-degree exchange at University of Wisconsin-Madison, September 2025 to May 2026. My studies there focused on finance, economics and financial modelling.

Before university, I studied Information Technology at SPŠ elektrotechnická Františka Křižíka. I also completed two short school IT training placements at Hewlett Packard Enterprise in Prague, in May 2022 and May 2023.

Beyond the screen

Mountains, aviation and travel are a big part of what I enjoy outside my studies. I like places and problems that reward preparation, curiosity and a clear head.
