About

I’m building toward quantitative finance through software, data, and financial modeling.

My current stage matters: I am a student with a clear direction, not a finished quantitative researcher. The site is designed to make that distinction obvious while showing the work I can already defend.

Current education

I study Informatics at the Faculty of Economics and Management, Czech University of Life Sciences Prague, with an expected bachelor’s completion in June 2027. The program gives me a foundation in programming, algorithms, computer architecture, logic, mathematics, and information systems.

From September 2025 to May 2026, I completed a non-degree exchange year at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. My completed coursework included finance, economics, financial modeling, quantitative methods, and operating systems.

Direction

I am interested in the intersection of market and risk questions with the software needed to analyze them carefully. That means improving probability and statistics, financial modeling, data quality, research design, and reproducible Python workflows—while continuing to build stronger systems skills.

How I work

I prefer a narrow question with visible assumptions over a broad claim that cannot be tested. Public projects state their status, data provenance, contribution boundary, validation attempts, and limitations. That is partly a communication choice and partly an engineering discipline.

Beyond the portfolio

Outside this work, I am interested in aviation, mountain environments, and the engineering systems behind both. Those interests stay secondary here because this website has one job: help a technical, recruiting, or admissions reader understand my current professional direction and inspect the evidence behind it.
