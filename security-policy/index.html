Security

Responsible disclosure for the public website.

Good-faith reports about davidanderle.com are welcome. Do not test private subdomains, accounts, email, third-party services, or data that you do not own.

In scope

The public static website at davidanderle.com, including public routes, downloads, redirects, and response-header behavior.

Out of scope

  • Denial-of-service or high-volume testing.
  • Social engineering, phishing, credential attacks, or physical access.
  • Testing against app.davidanderle.com or other access-controlled systems.
  • Third-party services such as GitHub, LinkedIn, the registrar, email providers, or Cloudflare itself.
  • Automated scanning that creates operational load.

How to report

Email david@davidanderle.com with the affected URL, impact, reproduction steps, and a safe proof of concept. Encrypting the report is optional; do not include secrets unless necessary.

Expectations

Acknowledge receipt when practical, preserve confidentiality while remediation is in progress, and coordinate public disclosure. This is a personal site, not a paid bug-bounty program.
